All anyone's talking about is the season  
Summer’s soon to say goodbye  
Trees turn orange, blooms run dry  
Early sunsets fade from view  
Fall is looming, peeking through  
I’ll use this time to reminisce  
On summer days I'll dearly miss

I remember the way the trees looked  
Green, brown, and growing tall  
Their canopy stood over all  
Imposing maples stretched across  
Fields with roots adorned by moss

I remember the way the flowers looked  
Marigolds with edges bright  
Lilies helping songs to write  
Forget-me-nots were everywhere  
Greenhouse tulips raised with care

I remember the way the sunsets looked  
Burning light would ever last  
Bleeding skies that never passed  
Orange faded baby blue  
Your little porch, a perfect view

I remember the way the days looked  
Summer solstice, smoking weed  
Projectors, people, sowing seeds  
See you soon, silver moon  
Singing Sunday morning tunes  
Trampolines, green canopies  
Marigolds and memories  
Weekend mornings, waking late  
Café drives, carrot cake  
Little tubes, little trips  
Cute little situations, dips  
Open mics, playing cards  
Throwing darts in Brian's yard  
Trulys warm and Trulys cold  
Throwing back and feeling old  
Snapping limbs, dancing late  
Mosquito bites, trusting fate

Already green is turning gold  
Morning air is growing cold  
Orange leaves, new things to miss  
Goldenrod, summer’s goodbye kiss

--Connor
