The camp is set and fire lit  
Gathered on some logs they sit  
Witches, bones, ghastly ghosts,  
and many other nasty hosts

Shadows long, no sun in sight  
The moon is casting wicked light  
Werewolves sing a jagged tune  
And warlocks spin their spells of doom

"Burn, Burn, Burn it down  
Halloween’s come back around  
Today we've built our appetite  
On humans we will feast tonight"

Frankenstein then rose up straight  
He just finished off his dinner plate  
He cleared his throat and bellowed loud  
Then all fell silent in the crowd

"Gather monsters far and wide  
I have a tale to tell tonight  
You may feel safe by the fireside  
But when I’m done you’ll be running to hide"

"Three cheers for Frankenstein  
We love a spooky story time  
Spin a tale that'll chatter teeth  
And shiver the souls that live beneath"

Once there was a Mummy-Man  
Who tried to start his mummy-van  
He turned the key and held his breath  
Then his heart went tumbling out of his chest

That evening was bitter cold  
And his battery was growing old  
The Mummy-Man forgot to turn off his lights  
And now his mummy-van won't start tonight

Ring ring the Mummy-Man  
Sent out a call to the Money-Man  
He let his wraps fall to his feet  
And begged for mercy in defeat

Hey hey Money-Man  
I hope that you will understand  
I know I'm late for work tonight  
But my mummy-van's not acting right

Mummy-Man O Mummy-Man  
You said that it wouldn’t happen again  
The last time it wasn't acting right  
You swore you'd have it fixed that night

Oh boy Money-Man  
I know I said it wouldn’t happen again  
I tried my best to get it right  
But I left my headlights on last night

Mummy-Man my Mummy-Man  
It's hard to run Hell without a doorman  
The sinners are stuck outside the gate  
No, the devils’ doorman can’t ever be late

Time and time I'll say it again  
It's hard to run Hell without a doorman  
I'm so done hearing all your lies  
So I'm sending you back to Earth to do a nine-to-five

The monsters then started running to hide  
There's no scarier thing than a nine-to-five  
The skeletons' bones were rattling free  
And the witches' brooms even tried to flee

Frankenstein wasn't telling a lie  
The mummy’s new name was Johnathan Rye  
Johnathan Rye's always checking his lights  
He'd never again run his battery dry

Johnathan Rye, Johnathan Rye  
The poor Mummy-Man’s now Johnathan Rye  
He's working hard at his nine-to-five  
And maybe if he's lucky he'll be soon to die

--Connor
