Often, it flows  
Through my fingertips  
Like water over a ledge,  
Spilling, abundant, and free.

But there’s always winter  
Somewhere, waiting,  
Setting storms to stack snowbanks  
Till the sun sends it streaming again.

All I ask is not to need a heat lamp  
To melt enough water  
To turn the turbines  
To power the heat lamp.

You never want to be thirsty  
During winter,  
Surrounded by water  
That's stuck in a block of ice.

--Connor
