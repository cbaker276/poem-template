Use your two cents to buy a cake mix  
Stir it in a pot  
Drop in the cookie however it crumbles  
And eat it too  
Have the hand that feeds you  
Serve it hot  
So you can bite it with revenge  
Then wait  
For the iron to get cold to strike again

If you can’t stand the heat in the kitchen  
Stay in the frying pan to avoid the fire  
Until you add fuel to your burning bridges  
And dam up the water under them  
Then fall through with your plans  
To whatever floats the same boat we’re all in  
Paddle it up the creek  
Then toss the paddle to wake sleeping dogs

Bark up the right tree  
Knocking on the wood  
Before you can try the apple  
As it falls far enough to poke the bear  
Then run from the hills  
You helped the moles make  
Jumping over the elephant in the room  
Straight to conclusions

I mean to beat around the bush  
When all’s not said and done  
Take this recipe made from disaster  
With more than a grain of salt  
As you can’t make an omelet  
Without breaking a few eggs

--Connor
