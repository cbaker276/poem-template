Miles away  
Miles away  
In my dreams I will find you  
Even miles away

These miles and miles  
Can’t keep us away  
From these dreams that we’re dreaming  
Wherever we stay

I’ll dream in our dreams  
When we’re dreaming apart  
Of a place with no miles  
Between where we are

I’ll dream that we drive  
These many miles apart  
Singing warm songs  
As the miles depart

When we’re deep in this dreaming  
No such thing as “too far”  
Our bodies share space  
Under skies full of stars

In my dreams I’ll be dreaming  
We’re not miles away  
Not a yard, not a foot  
Not an inch in our way

You are the place  
I’ll pick in my dreams  
No miles to measure  
No distance between

Let us sew up this space  
No more miles to speak of  
Only soft words to sing  
Sweet songs to be dreamt of

Sweet pie, sweet treat  
Whipped cream peaks made of stars  
You give me a sweet tooth  
From miles afar

Many miles and miles  
And miles there are  
So many miles between us  
Many miles apart

But that’s why we dream  
There’s no mileage between stars  
That could keep me from dreaming  
We’re not miles apart
